About Us

Learn more about Hearth & Grain Bakery.

Our Story

Hearth & Grain started as a small weekend bakery stall. Over time, it grew into a local bakery with its own shop.

For the past six years, we have been creating fresh sourdough bread, baked goods and custom celebration cakes.

Our Mission

Our mission is to bring people together through freshly baked food made with traditional methods and quality local ingredients.

Our Vision

We want to become a trusted bakery in our local community while using our website to reach more customers.
